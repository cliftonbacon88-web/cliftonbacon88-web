### Clifton Bacon

Independent researcher in upstate New York. Construction and engineering by trade, a daily meditation practice by choice, and one question I can't put down: **does the observer belong in the physics?**

I'm building **[The Silence Paradigm](https://github.com/cliftonbacon88-web/the-silence-paradigm)**, a pre-registered experiment that measures the coherence of a quantum sensor in diamond while a trained meditator, a metre away in a shielded room, moves from ordinary thinking down to complete silence.

Most experiments add energy to see what happens. This one takes it away.
